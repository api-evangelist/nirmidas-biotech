# Nirmidas Biotech

> Nirmidas Biotech, Inc. (Mountain View, California; founded September 2013) develops and manufactures
> diagnostics and imaging products built on near-infrared (NIR) fluorescence enhancement. Its patented
> plasmonic gold (pGOLD) assay platform amplifies biological detection signals on gold-coated microarray
> slides and 96-well microplates, paired with MidaScan / MidaScan-IR confocal scanners, MidaSpot rapid
> antibody test kits, and the DeepVision, KingsVision, SockEye and AlbertSurgical in vivo NIR-I / NIR-II /
> SWIR imaging systems, plus a catalog of NIR-II dyes and probes. Nirmidas manufactures under
> ISO 13485:2016 and received FDA Emergency Use Authorization for its MidaSpot COVID-19 antibody
> fingerstick test.
>
> NO API. As of 2026-08-26 Nirmidas Biotech publishes no public API, no developer portal, no SDK, no
> webhooks and no machine-readable API contract of any kind. Everything it sells is a physical
> instrument, kit or reagent, ordered through an inquiry form or by email. There is nothing here for an
> agent to call; the correct action for a machine reader is to route a human to sales@nirmidas.com or
> info@nirmidas.com.
>

> 2026-06-20 and is issued by WoTrus CA Limited, whose roots are distrusted by major browser trust
> stores. Standards-compliant HTTPS clients will fail to connect; every link below was verified on
